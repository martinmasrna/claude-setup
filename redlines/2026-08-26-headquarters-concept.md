# Headquarters — Concept

## What it is

The single place Martin runs his company from. Not a dashboard {~~— a dashboard~>that~~} {++*++}reports{++*++} on work happening elsewhere; this is where the work itself reaches him, gets judged, and visibly moves forward. Long term, every custom tool he builds (redline, the knowledge library, the tutor, one day his own agent harness) is a place inside{-- i--}t{++he headquarters++}{>>just a nit<<}, and it becomes the only surface he works from. It is for Martin alone.

## Why it exists

1. **Work should feel like play.** When work feels like a game, motivation is free, fifteen-hour days are a pleasure, and the output is better. Joy from the tool itself is {~~a~>one of the~~}{>>just a nit<<} design goal{++s++}, not {++just a ++}{>>just a nit<<}decoration.
2. **Martin's attention is the company's scarcest resource.** Agents pre-filter everything; only the highest-leverage judgments reach him, batched, in one place, instead of him patrolling files and apps.
3. **{~~Strict comparative advantage.~>Optimizing human <-> agent workflows.~~}{>>"Strict comparative advantage" doesn't explain AT ALL what the hell it's about. It's useless without reading the rest of pharagraph, therefore useless as a bold sub-heading.<<}** Every task belongs to whoever is better at it. Martin: product decisions, taste, his own writing, creative direction. Agents: everything else. Headquarters is where the hand-offs between the two happen.

## The core mechanic: turns

Agents work continuously; Martin plays turns. When he sits down, the game presents the state of every project, the queue of judgments that are ready for him, and what got built while he was away. He plays through the queue — each item opens the right file or tool directly — and the company advances. The product workflow's phases give each project a legible position, and its gates are where queue items come from.

## v0.001

The daily turn, on today's real company:

- Every project with its current phase, state, and what it's waiting on.
- The judgment queue, each item deep-linking into the right file or tool (redline pass ready, decision to approve, sample to inspect).
- What got built while Martin was away.

Out of scope: socials, clients, newsletter, calendar, metric integrations, talking to agents from inside it (unless it turns out trivially cheap).

## Exceptional vs. average

Exceptional: Martin opens it every morning because he wants to, his manual file-opens drop toward zero, and daily progress is something he can see and feel. Average: a status page — it reports instead of presenting, nothing on it actually needs him, and he quietly stops opening it within a week.
