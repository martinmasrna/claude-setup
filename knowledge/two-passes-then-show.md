Two self-directed polish passes on any artifact, then show it — a render, a proof page, a message. About to start a third pass without having shown anything is the signal to ship it for judgement instead. Measured from a git trail: 9 unprompted polish commits in 33 minutes drew zero comment from Martin, while feedback-driven changes converged the work. Options only need to be good enough to *judge*, not finished.
